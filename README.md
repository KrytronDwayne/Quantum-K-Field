K-Field: A Geometric Foundation for Physical Law

Because Earth’s position, motion, epoch, and observational state can be described in the same reference frame, the geometry of distant galaxies and the geometry of terrestrial measurement become directly comparable.

From this common frame, three plane systems emerge. Their intersections define a compact, oriented geometric cell known as Cell3. Cell3 is not an abstract symbol or a freely adjustable model. It is the corrected root geometry recovered from the astronomical data, preserving the orientation and internal relationships encoded by the galaxy-axis population. The larger volumetric and algebraic structure generated from Cell3 is called the Devon Dimension. Together, they form the foundation of the K-Field framework.

The significance of this foundation appears in what follows from it. Those same geometric quantities propagate into the Bohr radius, atomic velocity, Rydberg constant, Hartree energy, classical electron radius, vacuum impedance, and related physical quantities. A separate scalar branch produces the gravity structure, including the base gravitational scalar and its observer-dependent rendering. These are not isolated numerical matches. They are linked results descending from shared geometric origins.
