Investigator Biography

P. Dwayne Esterline is the Principal Investigator and founder of
Krytronx, LLC, an independent research organization focused exclusively
on the continued development of the K-field, a discovered geometric
foundation beneath all known physics. Through Krytronx, he directs a
long-term research program investigating structured geometry, geometric
generation, field-coupled behavior, and the deeper organization
underlying known physical branches.

Mr. Esterline is an independent investigator, self-taught physicist,
systems engineer, and polymath whose work integrates geometric
reasoning, experimental observation, engineering systems, software
development, instrumentation, manufacturing technology, and analytical
modeling into a unified investigative framework. Krytronx began with
applied manufacturing, IoT, analytics, and technology development, but
its mission has evolved into primary research in the fundamental
sciences centered on the K-field discovery.

Before founding Krytronx’s current research program, Mr. Esterline held
senior leadership positions across major industrial and automotive
organizations, including Tenneco Automotive, Ford Motor Company, and
Navistar International. As Director of Purchasing, he was responsible
for approximately $1 billion in annual spend across multiple North
American facilities, with staff based in Monroe, Michigan. His broader
career included executive leadership in purchasing, supplier
development, advanced engineering programs, manufacturing systems,
capital equipment, facilities projects, technical cost analysis,
automation, and operational strategy.

Mr. Esterline earned a Bachelor of Science in Organizational Management
from Huntington University in 1999, graduating with highest honors, and
a Master of Business Administration in Management and Accounting from
Indiana Wesleyan University in 2008.

His technical background spans programming and software development, CAD
and engineering design, database systems, laboratory instrumentation,
precision timing and OCXO characterization, sensor development, GPS
systems, thermal-control systems, robotics integration, automation, and
artificial intelligence model integration.

Today, Mr. Esterline directs the continuing development of the K-field
research program through Krytronx, LLC. The program focuses on the
geometric foundations of physical reality and the systematic generation
of downstream physical relationships from first geometric principles.
Current efforts include mathematical development, experimental
investigation, field-coupled technologies, and the continued expansion
of the K-field framework.
